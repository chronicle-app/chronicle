A visit to a post's page on X is the post, under any handle and with or without a photo open, so it merges with the post from a Twitter archive or the API, even when they don't know its author's handle. `webPage(url)` is a page by its address, and `sameAs` the post when it's one on X. `xPost(id)` is a post on X as the Twitter plugin keys it.
