A `Session` is one person's time, such as a night's sleep or their time on a call. Others who took part have sessions of their own, and something they shared, such as a concert or a meeting, is an `Event` that each session is part of. A `CallSession` is one person's time on a call. A `Journey` is a `Session`, so a trip has a planned period and stages like any other session.
