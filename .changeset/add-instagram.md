Add the Instagram plugin, bundled with the CLI. It reads an Instagram data export and emits your posts, stories, direct messages, likes, follows, saves, comments and searches. The vocabulary adds `Post`, `PublishAction`, `LikeAction`, `FollowAction`, `BookmarkAction`, `CreateAction`, `FindAction`, `RespondAction`, `Comment`, `Query`, `Response`, `sourceFormat`, `visibility`, `references`, and `target`.
