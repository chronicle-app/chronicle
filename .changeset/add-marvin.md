Add the Marvin plugin, bundled with the CLI. It reads Marvin's CSV exports and emits a `ReadAction` for each reading session and a `QuoteAction` or `AnnotateAction` for each highlight or note. The vocabulary adds `ReadAction`, `QuoteAction`, and `Quotation`.
